# ไฟล์นี้แสดงตัวอย่างการตั้งค่า Switch 
# Cisco รุ่น CBS350-24T-4G

# Reset switch
# วิธีที่ 1 The Web GUI:
Administration -> Reboot:
  Checked on: Restore to Factory Defaults
  Press "Reboot" button
# วิธีที่ 2 Physical Reset Button (Manual):
1.Locate the recessed Reset button (usually a small pinhole near the LED lights).
2.Using a paperclip or similar tool, press and hold the button.
3.To Reboot: Hold for less than 10 seconds.
4.To Factory Reset: Hold for more than 10 seconds.
5.Release the button. The System LED will likely flash, and the switch will reboot with the default configuration.
6.Default IP: 
  The router will typically default to 192.168.1.254


# Login to config
# วิธีที่ 1 Web browser:
1. Set your computer IP: 192.168.1.2, subnet mask:255.255.255.0 
2. Open on browser http://192.168.1.254

# วิธีที่ 2 Console:
1. Connect console cable between CBS350 switch and your computer
2. Open "Device Manager" to find your COM port (Port Com&LPT -> USB Serial port)
3. Open "PuTTY":
  Connection type: Serial
  Serial line: Checked from Device Manager (เช่น COM1)
  Speed: 115200
